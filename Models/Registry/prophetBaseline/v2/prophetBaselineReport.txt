INTERPRETATION AND LIMITATIONS
-------------------------------------------------------
MAPE is calculated only on days with non-zero actual sales; it is not directly comparable to MAPE calculated on all days.
WAPE is calculated over all holdout days.
Only the final 30 observed calendar days are held out. This is a single holdout and does not establish performance across multiple seasons.
The dataset ends in December 2011; this is a historical evaluation, not a current real-world forecast.
The target is eligible positive sales, not net sales after returns.
The 12% MAPE acceptance target is not considered met unless the reported result supports it under the project's agreed metric definition.
